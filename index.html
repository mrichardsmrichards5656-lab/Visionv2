Clear, accessible information for people living with sight conditions — and the people who support them.

Last updated: April 2026 · Reviewed quarterly ·

My daughter was diagnosed with Stargardt disease. When I started looking for help, I couldn't find one place that had everything — clear information, benefits guidance, research, and support — all in one place.

So I built it. And because sight loss affects so many people in so many different ways, I built it for everyone — not just for Stargardt, but for every eye condition.

This site is free, independent, and always will be.

📰 Recent Updates April 2026
Research

Stargardt: REGENXBIO reports encouraging Phase 2 interim data for RGX-314 dual-vector ABCA4 gene therapy — safety profile confirmed, efficacy readout expected later in 2026.

Research

Retinitis Pigmentosa: MeiraGTx RPGR gene therapy Phase 2/3 trial reports significant preservation of visual field in X-linked RP patients at 12-month follow-up.

Research

Usher Syndrome: ProQR's antisense oligonucleotide therapy for USH2A (QR-421a) shows continued visual function preservation at 24 months in ongoing trial.

Benefits

PIP & DLA rates updated April 2026 (3.8% rise): Daily Living enhanced rate £114.60/week. Mobility enhanced rate £80.00/week. Attendance Allowance higher rate £114.60/week. Carer's Allowance £86.45/week.

Technology

All conditions: Apple iOS 18 introduced significant VoiceOver improvements including enhanced image descriptions and improved braille display support.

Updates are verified and added each quarter. Information is for guidance only — always discuss research and treatment options with your ophthalmologist.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Plain English summary: Vision Hub does not sell your data. We do not collect personal information unless you choose to contact us via our contact form. We do not use advertising trackers.

👤 Who we are

Vision Hub is a free, independent UK resource for people living with partial sight and sight loss. You can contact us via the on this website.

📋 What information we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to run this website:

  • Contact form submissions: If you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address, and message. We use this only to respond to you.
  • Accessibility preferences: If you change text size or colour scheme, this is stored in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Feedback votes: If you click "Yes" or "No" on a helpful page rating, this is stored locally in your browser only. We do not collect this centrally.

🍪 Cookies

Vision Hub does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. We use browser local storage solely to remember your accessibility preferences between visits. This is stored on your own device and is not transmitted to us.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking technology.

🔗 Third-party links

Our website links to external sites including RNIB, Macular Society, Glaucoma UK, and the NHS. When you click these links, the destination website's privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for external sites.

We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log your IP address as part of this request. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

📞 The RNIB helpline number

When you tap the RNIB helpline number displayed on our site, your phone makes a direct call to RNIB. Vision Hub is not party to that call and does not record any information about it.

⚖️ Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

Where we process personal data (contact form submissions only), our legal basis is legitimate interests — specifically, responding to enquiries sent to us by individuals who have chosen to contact us. We do not process special category data.

🗂️ How long we keep your data

Contact form correspondence is retained for up to 12 months and then deleted, unless there is an ongoing matter requiring us to keep it longer.

🔒 Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

  • Object to our processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office)

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: April 2026

Our commitment: Vision Hub exists to serve people living with sight loss. Accessibility is not an afterthought — it is the core purpose of this website.

About this website

Vision Hub (visionhubuk.co.uk) is a free, independent UK resource for people living with partial sight, sight loss, and inherited eye conditions.

Conformance status

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standard, as required under the Equality Act 2010.

We believe this website is substantially conformant with WCAG 2.1 AA.

What we have built in

  • Text size controls ✓ Built in
  • High contrast mode ✓ Built in
  • Skip to main content link ✓ Built in
  • Keyboard navigation ✓ Built in — Press Escape to close any open panel.
  • Screen reader support ✓ Built in
  • Minimum 44×44px tap targets ✓ Built in
  • Colour contrast ✓ Passes AA
  • Readable font ✓ Built in — 17px base size with 1.7 line height.
  • Preference memory ✓ Built in
  • Accessible contact form ✓ Built in — labelled fields, error messages, and success confirmation.

Known limitations

  • An independent WCAG audit has not yet been commissioned.

Assistive technology tested

  • VoiceOver on iPhone (iOS 17)
  • TalkBack on Android
  • NVDA on Windows with Chrome
  • Keyboard-only navigation on Chrome, Firefox, and Safari
  • 200% browser zoom

Reporting accessibility problems

Use our to report any accessibility barriers. We aim to respond within 5 working days.
