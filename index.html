Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Plain English summary: Vision Hub does not sell your data. We do not collect personal information unless you choose to contact us by email. We do not use advertising trackers.

👤 Who we are

Vision Hub is a free, independent UK resource for people living with partial sight and sight loss. We can be contacted at mrichardsmrichards5656@gmail.com.

📋 What information we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to run this website:

  • Email contact: If you email us, we receive your email address and message content. We use this only to respond to you.
  • Accessibility preferences: If you change text size or colour scheme, this is stored in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Feedback votes: If you click "Yes" or "No" on a helpful page rating, this is stored locally in your browser only. We do not collect this centrally.

🍪 Cookies

Vision Hub does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. We use browser local storage solely to remember your accessibility preferences between visits. This is stored on your own device and is not transmitted to us.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking technology.

🔗 Third-party links

Our website links to external sites including RNIB, Macular Society, Glaucoma UK, and the NHS. When you click these links, the destination website's privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for external sites.

We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log your IP address as part of this request. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

📞 The RNIB helpline number

When you tap the RNIB helpline number displayed on our site, your phone makes a direct call to RNIB. Vision Hub is not party to that call and does not record any information about it.

⚖️ Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

Where we process personal data (email correspondence only), our legal basis is legitimate interests — specifically, responding to enquiries sent to us by individuals who have chosen to contact us. We do not process special category data.

🗂️ How long we keep your data

Email correspondence is retained for up to 12 months and then deleted, unless there is an ongoing matter requiring us to keep it longer.

🔒 Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to our processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office)

To exercise any of these rights, email mrichardsmrichards5656@gmail.com.

You can contact the ICO at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

📝 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will always reflect when it was last changed.

✉️ Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email mrichardsmrichards5656@gmail.com.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: April 2026

Our commitment: Vision Hub exists to serve people living with sight loss. Accessibility is not an afterthought — it is the core purpose of this website.

About this website

Vision Hub (visionhubuk.co.uk) is a free, independent UK resource for people living with partial sight, sight loss, and inherited eye conditions. This statement explains how accessible our website is and what we are doing to improve it.

Conformance status

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standard, as required under the Equality Act 2010.

We believe this website is substantially conformant with WCAG 2.1 AA. This means most — but not all — of the criteria are fully met.

What we have built in

  • Text size controls ✓ Built in — Four text size options accessible at the top of every page.
  • High contrast mode ✓ Built in — White-on-black and yellow-on-black colour schemes.
  • Skip to main content link ✓ Built in — Appears on focus for keyboard and screen reader users.
  • Keyboard navigation ✓ Built in — All interactive elements reachable by keyboard. Press Escape to close any open panel.
  • Screen reader support ✓ Built in — ARIA labels, roles, and aria-expanded states throughout.
  • Minimum 44×44px tap targets ✓ Built in — All buttons and links meet the minimum size.
  • Colour contrast ✓ Passes AA — All text meets the 4.5:1 contrast ratio requirement.
  • Readable font ✓ Built in — 17px base size with 1.7 line height.
  • Preference memory ✓ Built in — Text size and colour scheme remembered between visits.

Known limitations

We are aware of the following issues and are working to address them:

  • Some condition panels are not yet fully populated and display placeholder text.
  • Some condition panels are not yet fully populated and display placeholder text.
  • An independent WCAG audit has not yet been commissioned.

Assistive technology tested

  • VoiceOver on iPhone (iOS 17)
  • TalkBack on Android
  • NVDA on Windows with Chrome
  • Keyboard-only navigation on Chrome, Firefox, and Safari
  • 200% browser zoom

How to use accessibility features on your device

iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size. Enable Larger Text, Bold Text, and Increase Contrast.

Android: Settings → Accessibility → Vision. Enable large text and high contrast text.

Screen readers: VoiceOver is built into iPhone. TalkBack is built into Android via Settings → Accessibility → TalkBack.

Reporting accessibility problems

We want to hear from you if you experience any accessibility barriers on this website. Email: mrichardsmrichards5656@gmail.com

We aim to respond within 5 working days.

Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our response to an accessibility complaint, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS):

  • Website: equalityadvisoryservice.com
  • Phone: 0808 800 0082

What we are working on next

  • Adding full content to all condition panels
  • Commissioning an independent WCAG audit
  • Adding a print stylesheet for saving information as PDF
